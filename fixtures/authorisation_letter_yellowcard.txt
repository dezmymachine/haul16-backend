APPOINTMENT OF DESIGNATED AGENT:
Yellow Card Ghana Limited hereby formally appoints and designates HAUL16 (Hall 16 Brand
Protection & Takedown Desk) as its authorized agent to detect, monitor, investigate, document,
and enforce brand abuse, trademark infringement, malicious lookalike domains, phishing attacks,
fraudulent cryptocurrency schemes, and unauthorized mobile money cash collection targeting
Yellow Card and its users in the Republic of Ghana and West Africa.

HAUL16 is fully empowered to submit takedown notices, request domain suspensions, seek network
blacklisting, demand de-indexing, and log fraud complaints on behalf of Yellow Card Ghana Limited
pursuant to:
- Section 92 of the Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772);
- Section 35 of the Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038);
- Non-Bank Financial Institutions / Payment Systems and Services Guidelines of Ghana.
